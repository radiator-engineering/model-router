## Privacy edits and provenance

The initial report's claim about the user's relative Claude/OpenAI allowance has been replaced with a configurable resource-policy statement. The follow-up's personal allowance ratio has been replaced with a statement about differing provider allowances. No benchmark, compatibility, or pricing claim was changed during this privacy edit.

Untouched originals are retained in ignored `/local/r-and-d/` on the originating workstation. That directory is optional and is not part of a public clone. Treat the files here as redacted source copies rather than verbatim originals.

The [v2 specs](../README.md) use the follow-up's smaller decision tree as the design direction. Claims about models, benchmarks, prices, APIs, and workstation behavior need evidence appropriate to their use. The research authors did not establish the installed harness and proxy's capabilities through local experiments.

Add future research as separate files. Record the question, source or experiment, date, exact environment where relevant, result, limitation, and affected spec section. Generalize personal account details before publication. Original research remains an input, even when later findings supersede a claim.
